            How it works

            Search for a worship song by title or artist — the site looks it up for you — then tap Add to save it to My Songs. Tap Play on any song to hear it in the player above; each song plays as a 30-second preview. The Spotify Liked Songs tab holds the worship songs from Eric’s Spotify library — play or shuffle them right there, or add any of them to My Songs or a playlist.

            Create as many playlists as you like with Create playlist, tap a playlist to open it, and fill it with Add to playlist — songs go into whichever playlist is open. Arrange a playlist with the up and down arrows, rename or delete it whenever you like, and press Play playlist — each song plays through in order, and the next one starts on its own when the current song ends. Your songs and playlists save automatically in this browser. Use Export JSON for an exact backup you can restore with Import JSON, or Export CSV for a spreadsheet-friendly copy.
