        How it works

        Search for a worship song by title or artist — the site looks it up for you — then tap Add to save it to My Songs. Tap Play on any song to hear it in the player above; each song plays as a 30-second preview.

        Build a playlist with Add to playlist, arrange it with the up and down arrows, and press Play playlist — each song plays through in order, and the next one starts on its own when the current song ends. The songs and playlist on this page are shared, so everyone who visits sees the same lists.
